Phone Setup Safety & Multi-Device Protection

When connecting a phone or new device, Recovery Tracker uses a Pull-First Policy. It fetches your cloud data first to prevent a new device from wiping your Google Sheet.

  • Who has access: Anyone

About Recovery Tracker

Version 2.4.0

Your Personal Medical & Recovery Companion

Recovery Tracker manages medication schedules, interval compliance, Web Push Notifications, and 4 surgical drain outputs.

  • 1
    Web Push Notifications & Reminders (v2.4.0)

    Click 🔔 Remind Me on any As-Needed medication during its cooldown. When the countdown hits 0, you'll receive a native browser push notification!

  • 2
    Scheduled Dose Time Windows

    Scheduled daily doses match exact window boundaries: Morning (7 AM – 12 PM), Afternoon (12 PM – 4 PM), Evening (4 PM – 8 PM), and Night (8 PM – 2 AM).

  • 3
    Medical Drain Output Tracking (4 Drains)

    Record output fluid volume (ml) and fluid character for Drains 1, 2, 3, and 4.
