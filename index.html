About Recovery Tracker

Version 1.0.0

Your Personal Medical & Dose Compliance Companion

Recovery Tracker is designed to make managing medications effortless, safe, and transparent whether hosted locally or on GitHub Pages.

  • 1
    Tracking "As-Needed" Medications

    As-Needed medications specify a minimum interval (e.g. every 4 hours or more). When you log a dose, the card turns RED during the cooldown period and displays a live countdown. Once the minimum interval elapses, the card turns GREEN signalizing it is safe to take again.

  • 2
    Tracking "Scheduled" Medications

    Scheduled medications are assigned to specific daily time slots: Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Night. You can mark them off directly from the Dashboard or the Daily Schedule checklist.

  • 3
    Logging Doses & Notes

    Click "Take Dose" or "Log Dose" on any medication card to log exact timestamp, quantity, and notes (such as symptoms, meal context, or pain level).

  • 4
    Reports & Doctor Exports

    Navigate to the Logs & Reports tab to view your complete timeline, filter by date ranges, export CSV data, or generate a formatted printout for your doctor appointments.
